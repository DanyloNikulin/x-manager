# Reply playbook

No reply playbook is configured. Create an untracked `playbook.md` (or edit the Playbook
field in the Account console) that tells the writer how to triage what comes in:

- the classes of inbound mentions this account sees (question, pushback, agreement, praise,
  bait, spam, hostile, ...) and, per class, whether to answer, ignore, or escalate to the
  operator;
- what needs a source in a reply, how long an answer should be, and when to stop;
- who is never answered (bots, farms, anyone the strategy marks as needing review).

The depth cap (how many replies in one chain) is a switch in the console, not text here.
