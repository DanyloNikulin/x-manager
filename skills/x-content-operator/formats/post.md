# Format: single post

One post, one idea, developed: the claim, the mechanism or evidence behind it, and the consequence or the twist. One paragraph, or two short ones separated by a blank line. `tweets` stays empty.

## Budget

- Hard limit: 280 weighted characters. A URL always counts as 23, whatever its length.
- Working band: 200 to 280. The worker measures every draft; a post under 200 comes back for a rewrite with the measured number. Write toward 240 to 270 and stop.
- Short sentences are the voice. Short posts are not. Fill the box with substance, never with sentences that restate the first one.

## Structure that fills the box

1. The claim, stated flat: what the account thinks.
2. The mechanism or the evidence: how it works, or the number with its unit, what it is a share of, and its source URL when the brief requires sourced numbers.
3. The consequence, the twist, or the one-line verdict. The twist is the last sentence. Do not explain it.

A post that reads as an aphorism has not used its budget. Two aphorisms taped together have not either. When the task material honestly supports only one line, give the fullest honest version, say so in `rationale`, and do not pad.

## What counts as substance

- The specific thing by name: the auction, the bill, the model, the tool. Not the category.
- One concrete number with its unit and what it is a share of.
- The step everyone skips: who pays, who presses the button, what the number excludes.
- The account's read on why it matters, in its own register.

## What does not count

- Restating the claim in other words, a moral, a question to the reader, hedges, "in other words".
- Explaining the joke. An "as a ..." frame.
- Hashtags, emoji, calls to action.
- Sentences that exist only to reach the number.

## The brief's samples

Sample posts in the brief show temperature and diction, not length. Match their temperature, ignore their length: the band above wins on length, the brief wins on voice.
